# Incident Review Packet

## Definition

An incident review packet is a compact document created after an outage, escalation, or operational event. It turns raw evidence such as recordings, transcripts, alerts, and chat logs into a readable record of what happened, what the team decided, and what still needs follow-up.

## Context and Usage

Engineering teams use incident review packets to keep post-incident reviews grounded in evidence.
A good packet usually includes a short summary, timeline, decisions made during the call, open questions, action items, and links to the original recording or transcript.
It is not a replacement for the review meeting. It gives the meeting a shared starting point.
